Submission deadlines

Every track we are targeting between now and the end of 2027, on one timeline. Each dot is a submission deadline, with its date beside it. Where a track has a separate abstract deadline, the thin line runs from the abstract to the paper. The faint marks after a deadline are notification and camera-ready; the grey block is the event itself. Dates are the venues' own, except the ones tagged projected, which repeat last year's date until the new call is out.

Dates checked 14 November 2026. Anything tagged projected comes from the previous cycle because the 2027 call is not published yet — confirm on the venue site before planning around it.

IMWUT is a journal. New papers go in on 1 February, 1 May and 1 November; 1 August takes major-revision resubmissions only. IMWUT publishes rules rather than dates, so each cycle’s row shows the dates those rules give: a first decision about eight weeks after the deadline, minor revisions due within four weeks of it, a major revision resubmitted at one of the next two deadlines, and a second decision about eight weeks after that (the tooltip gives the later option too). Accepted IMWUT papers are presented at UbiComp/ISWC, which runs 3–7 October 2027 in Seoul. Tracks whose deadline has passed are greyed out and marked closed; they stay on the chart so the review and revision milestones that follow them remain visible.

Each submission is one row, with all its dates as marks on the same line: internal deadlines (purple rings), abstract, the deadline itself, notification, a second-round revision (cyan) with its final notification, and camera-ready. Hover a mark for what it is; hover elsewhere on the row for every date at once. Different tracks of one conference (full papers, posters, demos) stay separate rows. The event column is the whole conference unless the venue gives a track its own days, as DIS does for workshops.

DIS runs a Provocations and Works-in-Progress track most years, but it is not in the 2027 call yet, so it is not on the chart.

EuroVis 2027 has published dates for full papers, STARs, short papers and workshop proposals. Its panels, tutorials, posters and demos calls are not out yet (their pages are still empty), so those rows repeat EuroVis 2026 dates and have no notification yet. EuroVis 2026 also ran an Education Papers track (12 January), but it is not in the 2027 call yet, so it is not on the chart.

The Destination Horizon comprehensive and administrative reviews, and the Knowledge Synthesis Grant administrative review (19 November) and final hand-off (1 December), are Ontario Tech internal deadlines set by the research office, ahead of SSHRC’s own deadlines (22 January and 3 December). Both applications go through the Convergence Portal, and every project director and co-applicant needs a tri-agency CV. Most funders give only a month for results, so the chart puts those marks at the end of that month: NFRF-E by 31 March 2027, both SSHRC grants in March 2027, Sony “around March 2027”, and Amazon in February 2027 (its decision letters go out about three months after the 11 November deadline). Destination Horizon grants are chosen by random selection among eligible applications, not by peer review.
