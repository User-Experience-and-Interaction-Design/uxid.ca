Deadlines

This page is for UXID Lab members. Enter the lab password to see it.

Submission deadlines

Every track we are targeting between now and the end of 2027, on one timeline. Each dot is a submission deadline, with its date beside it. Where a track has a separate abstract deadline, the thin line runs from the abstract to the paper. The faint marks after a deadline are notification and camera-ready; the grey block is the event itself. Dates are the venues' own, except the ones tagged projected, which repeat last year's date until the new call is out.

Dates checked 23 October 2026. Anything tagged projected comes from the previous cycle because the 2027 call is not published yet — confirm on the venue site before planning around it.

IMWUT is a journal with fixed quarterly intake. It promises decisions about eight weeks after each deadline rather than on a set date, so it has no notification mark, and its 1 August slot takes major-revision resubmissions only, not new submissions. Accepted IMWUT papers are presented at UbiComp/ISWC, which runs 3–7 October 2027 in Seoul. Tracks whose deadline has passed are greyed out and marked closed; they stay on the chart so the review and revision milestones that follow them remain visible.

Where a track has a second review round, the revision deadline gets its own row (“revisions, second round”), with the final notification and camera-ready after it. The first-round notification stays on the original row. The event column is the whole conference unless the venue gives a track its own days, as DIS does for workshops.

DIS runs a Provocations and Works-in-Progress track most years, but it is not in the 2027 call yet, so it is not on the chart.

EuroVis 2027 has only published dates for STARs and workshop proposals so far. Its other tracks are projected from EuroVis 2026 in Nottingham, so they have no notification dates yet. EuroVis 2026 also ran an Education Papers track (12 January), but it is not in the 2027 call yet, so it is not on the chart.

The Destination Horizon comprehensive and administrative reviews are Ontario Tech internal deadlines set by the research office, ahead of SSHRC’s 22 January deadline. The full application goes through the Convergence Portal, and every project director and co-applicant needs a tri-agency CV.
