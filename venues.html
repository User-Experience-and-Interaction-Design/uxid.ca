Which venue for which area

Built from the area tags in the workbook. The first line is where the workbook marks that area a core topic (gold is Tier 1). Generalist venues like CHI, DIS and UIST are tagged as partial on nearly every area rather than core, so the Tier 1 venues that also cover it are listed underneath.

AreaVenues that treat it as a core topic

Source: in your iCloud Conference Deadlines folder. The page is generated from that workbook, so the spreadsheet stays the one place you edit; re-export and the page follows.

Fit is computed, not from the workbook. Your Fit_Score column is empty, so the page derives it: the area tags the workbook gives each venue, weighted towards the lab's own topics (smartwatch 1.0, mobile 0.8, human-AI 0.8, visualization 0.6, accessibility 0.5; a “partial” tag counts half), plus the HCI relevance rating, all multiplied by a tier factor so a weak venue tagged across many areas cannot outrank a strong one. The result is scaled so the closest-fitting venue in the list scores 5 — 5 means best fit here, not perfect fit. Fill in Fit_Score in the spreadsheet and the page will use your number instead.

Attendance comes from the workbook's presentation mode. “In person*” means in person with remote allowed only by exception; the exact wording is in the expanded row. Pages and acceptance are shortened in the table and shown in full there too.

On the passcode: it is checked in the browser, so anyone who views the page source can read it, and the data file sits at a plain URL beside the page. It keeps this out of casual sight and out of search results; it is not real access control.

Not every row is dated. Rolling journals show Rolling and tracks whose call is not out yet show not announced; both stay visible under “upcoming only”. Venues the workbook marks discontinued are left out.
